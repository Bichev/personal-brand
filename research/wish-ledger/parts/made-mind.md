## Checking notes

- Iliad 18.417-420, A. T. Murray (1924, public domain), read on Perseus. Composition date: late 8th to early or mid 7th century BCE (Wikipedia, Iliad).
- Dartmouth: the proposal is dated 31 August 1955 and is "credited with introducing the term". Four authors, so the article does not say McCarthy alone coined it.
- Turing test study: keep the qualifier that the model was prompted to adopt a humanlike persona.
- METR, 20 November 2026: 50% time horizon of 40 hours for GPT-6 Astra (95% CI 13 to 2,645) and 39 hours for Opus 5.5 (95% CI 12 to 1,253). No 80% figure was found in the post. March 2025 post: about one hour for Claude 3.7 Sonnet.
- Home robots: based on the 1X Wikipedia article and the tracker at humanoidsforhome.com (checked 1 November 2026), which lists no independently verified NEO delivery. No primary confirmation. Reword if a verified delivery appears.
- Wiener: sentence read in a reproduction of the paper, not in Science itself. Citation given elsewhere as Science 131 (3410), 6 May 1960, pp. 1355-1358, not confirmed.
- ChatGPT: 900 million weekly users, February 2026 (Wikipedia). A later 1 billion figure was reported as imminent in July 2026 and is not used.
